# qPdf2MD 隱私權政策 / Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective date: 2026-10-21

## 中文

qPdf2MD（以下稱「本程式」）由 SAISAI 開發，是在你的電腦上執行的 PDF 轉 Markdown 工具。

**我們不收集任何個人資料。**

- **檔案只在你的電腦上處理。** 你加入的 PDF、轉換出的 Markdown 與圖片，都只在本機讀取與寫入，存放在你選擇的資料夾。本程式不會上傳、傳送或備份任何檔案內容。
- **完全離線執行。** 版面分析與文字辨識（OCR）的模型都隨程式安裝，轉換時不需要、也不會連線到網路。
- **沒有帳號、廣告、分析或遙測。** 本程式不使用 Cookie、追蹤器或任何第三方分析服務。
- **本機設定與錯誤記錄。** 為了記住你的偏好（例如主題、輸出位置、上次開啟的資料夾），本程式會在你的電腦上儲存設定；程式發生未預期錯誤時，錯誤訊息會寫入本機的記錄檔。這些資料不會離開你的電腦；Microsoft Store 版解除安裝時，Windows 會一併清除。
- **PDF 密碼。** 輸入的 PDF 開啟密碼只在記憶體中用來解鎖該檔案，不會儲存。
- **外部連結。** 只有在你點選「關於」視窗中的連結（例如電子郵件或原始碼網址）時，才會由你的瀏覽器或郵件程式開啟；之後適用該網站或服務的隱私權政策。
- **下載與更新。** 透過 Microsoft Store 下載與更新時，相關資料由 Microsoft 處理，適用 [Microsoft 隱私權聲明](https://privacy.microsoft.com/)。Microsoft 會依你的 Windows 診斷資料設定，向開發者提供彙總的下載統計與程式當機報告；本程式本身不會傳送任何資料。

若本政策日後變更，我們會更新此頁面與生效日期。若本程式未來加入任何需要傳送資料的功能，會在推出前更新此政策並在程式中清楚告知。

## English

qPdf2MD ("the app") is developed by SAISAI. It is a PDF-to-Markdown converter that runs on your computer.

**We do not collect any personal information.**

- **Your files stay on your computer.** The PDFs you add and the Markdown and images the app produces are read and written locally, in folders you choose. The app does not upload, transmit or back up any file content.
- **Fully offline.** The layout-analysis and text-recognition (OCR) models are installed with the app; conversion does not need or use a network connection.
- **No accounts, ads, analytics or telemetry.** The app uses no cookies, trackers or third-party analytics services.
- **Local settings and error log.** To remember your preferences (such as theme, output location and last folder), the app stores settings on your computer. If an unexpected error occurs, its details are written to a local log file. This data never leaves your computer; Windows clears it when you uninstall the Microsoft Store version.
- **PDF passwords.** A password you enter is used in memory only to open that file and is never stored.
- **External links.** Links in the About box (such as the e-mail address or source code URL) open in your browser or mail app only when you click them; the privacy policy of that site or service then applies.
- **Downloads and updates.** Downloads and updates through the Microsoft Store are processed by Microsoft under the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/). Depending on your Windows diagnostic data settings, Microsoft provides developers with aggregated download statistics and crash reports; the app itself sends no data.

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If a future version adds any feature that sends data, we will update this policy before release and tell you clearly in the app.
